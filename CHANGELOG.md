# Changelog

## 1.2.1

- Add a separate **Use Custom Dusk/Dawn Colors** toggle for Caribbean Turquoise, Emerald Sea, Winter Aestrin, and Open Chronos Ocean. Each defaults to On and saves independently.
- Turn a preset's toggle Off to use vanilla clear dawn/dusk ocean colors while retaining its custom clear-day colors. Existing time, weather, and region transitions continue blending normally.
- Give Winter Aestrin a muted slate-blue dawn/dusk palette: water `#385F83`, surface `#71869E`, and scattering `#4F7E9F`.
- Preserve existing preset selections, region assignments, master switch, plugin GUID, and config path.
